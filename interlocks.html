Cross-domain correlation layer

Logan across every interlock.

Oracle Log Analytics, called Logan in this field guide, now appears throughout network, security, IAM, landing-zone, operations, and end-to-end workflows. It turns mixed operational records into searchable evidence, clusters recurring patterns, links related activity, and supports scheduled detections.

  1. Logs and eventsIngest file, syslog, database, Windows event, REST, OCI service, Streaming, Object Storage, and OpenTelemetry log records.
  2. MetricsExtract measures from log records or correlate them with OCI Monitoring using shared time, entity, and workload labels.
  3. PrometheusPrometheus exporter metrics remain in a metric backend. Pair them with Logan records rather than describing Logan as a Prometheus scraper.
  4. APMAPM remains a linked service in current public documentation. Correlate traces and logs with trace context; treat any future consolidation as roadmap-dependent.
